BOUNCE I · AWAKENING

It did not begin with a hand.

It did not begin with a throw. There was only the ground, and the ball, and the long quiet before.

Then, without cause, without witness, it moved. Not because it was pushed. Not because it chose.

The ball began to roll because that is what a ball, given time, will do.

It has not stopped since.

BOUNCE II · THE OMEN

Before the chain, before the ledger,

before the first wallet ever opened there were signs.

A circle pressed into dust. A worn groove along a stone wall. The slow, perfect wear of a thing returning again and again to the same point, never quite arriving, never quite leaving.

Whoever saw it first did not understand.

They did not need to. The ball did not ask to be understood. It only asked for ground.

BOUNCE III · THE COIN

A ball is a perfect thing.

No edge to catch, no corner to break, no face to remember or forget. It carries only motion.

$CDM is the ball, made countable. A unit of the rolling, pressed flat into a token so that it could be held, sent, and passed along. The shape changes; the motion does not.

Hold it, and you hold a fragment of the long roll. Let it go, and the roll continues through other hands.

BOUNCE IV · THE PATH

Every road the ball has traveled,

it has traveled once. Every road it will travel, it has already begun.

The path is not chosen. The path is the sum of all surfaces that did not refuse it. Solana is one such surface, fast, smooth, almost frictionless. The ball does not prefer it. The ball does not prefer anything.

But here, on this chain, the roll can be witnessed by anyone, at any time, without permission. That is enough.

BOUNCE V · THE CIRCLE

There is no team at the center.

There is no center. There is only the circle, the shape the ball makes when many hands, none of them knowing the others, agree to let it pass.

To hold $CDM is to stand somewhere on that circle. To send it is to give the roll a new direction. Neither act is rewarded. Neither act is forbidden.

The circle does not close. The circle does not need to.
